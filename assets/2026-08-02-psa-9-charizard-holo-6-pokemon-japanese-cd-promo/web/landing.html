The story

Never sold in a pack. Ever.

The "Pokémon Song Best Collection" CD released in Japan in 1998, bundling the anime's soundtrack with a small set of promo cards. Card No. 006 was this Charizard — Ken Sugimori's original art on a vintage Japanese old-back holo.

No boosters. No reprints. If you wanted this Charizard, you bought the music. Twenty-seven years later, the surviving mint copies are the ones that were never played with — and this one earned a PSA MINT 9.
