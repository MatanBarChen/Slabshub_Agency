# Video Storyboard — Slab Guard Launch

Format: vertical Reel/Story, 10–15s, Hebrew on-screen text (RTL), Instagram/Facebook.
Concept: colors as the hero — a fast, satisfying "color drop" through the fan, into a value beat, into CTA.
Note: we do not yet have footage of the guard actually snapping onto a slab (see image-spec.md "Missing asset").
This cut works entirely from the two real product photos + `promo-main.png`; once Matan shoots the on-slab /
hand-snap shots, re-cut shot 1 around that footage — it will hit harder than the zoom-in used here.

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Hook (0:00–0:02, ~2s)
- **Visual:** fast push-in zoom on `promo-main.png` (solid-color fan), starting wide on the dark studio scene
  and snapping to a tight crop on the color fan on a hard beat. Quick flash/pulse of light across the frames
  as it lands (reads as a "reveal", not a card slam — this is an accessory, not a grail).
- **On-screen text:** **"שישה צבעים חדשים הגיעו"** (6 new colors arrived) — appears on the snap.
- **Transition out:** whip-pan left into shot 2.

### Shot 2 — Detail (0:02–0:08, ~6s)
- **Visual:** slow lateral pan across the solid-color fan, left to right, each frame catching a soft rim-light
  glow as the camera passes it (pink → purple → blue → mint → yellow → red). At ~0:05 hard-cut to the
  glitter/gradient photo, camera pushes in slowly on the sparkle detail/gradient transition.
- **On-screen text (2 beats):**
  - 0:02–0:05: **"לכל קלף הצבע שלו"** (every card gets its color)
  - 0:05–0:08: **"קולקציית הגליטר הפרימיום"** (the premium glitter collection)
- **Transition out:** quick cut to value screen.

### Shot 3 — Value (0:08–0:12, ~4s)
- **Visual:** clean dark card/graphic (near-black #0B0B0D background, gold accent) — two price pills animate in
  side by side: "₪19" (standard, labeled "צבעים") and "₪22" (labeled "גליטר"). A third smaller badge drops in
  below: the automatic 3+ discount.
- **On-screen text:**
  - Price pills: **"₪19"** / **"₪22"**
  - Promo badge: **"קונים 3+ = 15% הנחה"**
- **Transition out:** gold accent line wipes to CTA card.

### Shot 4 — CTA (0:12–0:15, ~3s)
- **Visual:** SlabsHub wordmark/logo centered on the same dark studio background, small color-fan graphic
  echo underneath for continuity.
- **On-screen text:** **"לינק בביו · Shop Now"**
- **Hold on last frame** to 0:15.

## Music / mood
Trending-audio friendly — leave the audio slot open for Matan to drop in a current trending sound. Mood
reference: upbeat, punchy, snappy cuts on the beat (think product-reveal / unboxing energy, not cinematic/
dramatic like the grail-card cuts). Tempo should support a hard hit on the 0:00 reveal and the 0:08 price beat.
