# Video Prompts — Slab Guard Launch (Veo / Google Flow)

Matan generates these himself in Flow (labs.google/flow) using image-to-video, with a product photo as the
start frame. Veo clips render at 8s each — generate the full 8s per shot, then trim in the edit to match the
storyboard's shorter durations (see `video-storyboard.md`).

**Start frames to download and upload to Flow:**
- Shot 1 & Shot 2a: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0622/2069/7711/files/slab-guard-solid-colors_9f4e39d2-6dea-45a5-9eaf-c2cb9ea945ac.jpg
- Shot 2b: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0622/2069/7711/files/slab-guard-glitter-gradient_6d8631f2-bca4-4737-b2cb-26c4c0f5c629.jpg
- Shot 3 & Shot 4: no start frame — generate from text prompt only (graphic/logo card, not a product photo).

## Shot 1 — Hook

**Start frame:** slab-guard-solid-colors.jpg

**Prompt:**
"A macro product photo of six colorful silicone card-slab protector frames (hot pink, purple, sky blue, mint
green, yellow, red) fanned out on a dark leather surface. Camera starts on a wide shot of the whole dark scene,
then pushes in fast with a sharp, punchy zoom toward the fan of colored frames, landing on a tight close-up.
As the camera lands, a quick flash of soft studio light sweeps across the frames like a reveal moment. Fast,
energetic camera movement, snappy timing, shallow depth of field on the landing shot. Studio lighting, near-black
background throughout, high contrast, premium commercial product-photography look. No text, no logos, no people,
no additional objects. Duration: 8 seconds."
