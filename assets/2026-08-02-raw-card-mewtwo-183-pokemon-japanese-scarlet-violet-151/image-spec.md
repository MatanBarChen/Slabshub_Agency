# Image Spec — RAW Mewtwo AR 183/165 (Japanese 151 / SV2a)

**Campaign date:** 2026-08-02

## Visual concept

RAW card = the artwork itself is the hero. No slab, no grading label — so instead of the
gold/label treatment, we lean into Mewtwo's identity: near-black background charged with
**purple/blue psychic-energy glow** and subtle bokeh, product-photography rim light around
the card, faint floor reflection. Premium, moody, collector-grade. The neon-city full-art
does the selling; text stays minimal.

## Placements

### IG feed — 1080x1350 (4:5) — primary
- Card centered, occupying ~62% of frame height, vertical, slight rim glow.
- Background: #0b0714 base, radial purple (#7b3fe4) and blue (#3b6bd6) energy wisps behind
  the card, small warm bokeh dots at low opacity in corners.
- Text overlay (top safe zone, above card): hook, max 6 words —
  **"Mewtwo AR. $19.99. Under market."**
- Price badge: bottom-right, pill, purple gradient border on dark fill: **$19.99** with
  micro-label "market $24.59" beneath (small, honest, dated source in caption not on image).
- Small "RAW · 1 IN STOCK" tag bottom-left, uppercase, letterspaced, 60% white.

### IG story — 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Same background system, card at ~55% height, centered slightly above middle.
- Top text: **"Under-market Mewtwo."** Bottom third: **"$19.99 — only one."** + link sticker
  zone left clear (bottom 250px).

### FB ad / square — 1080x1080 (1:1)
- Card shifted left-of-center at ~70% height; right column stacks: hook line,
  price badge, "Shop now" ghost button. Same dark psychic background.

## Rules applied
- Max 6 words per text overlay.
- No Pokemon logos or extra IP — only the product photo itself.
- Do NOT claim NM condition or grade potential on-image (condition unverified per brief);
  the grading-ladder numbers ($125 PSA 10) live in the copy/caption, dated, not on the visual.

## Generated asset — promo-main.png

- Generated 2026-08-02 via Gemini (gemini-3.1-flash-image), image-to-image from the Shopify
  product photo. 4:5 vertical, dark purple/blue psychic glow + bokeh, rim light, reflection,
  clear space at top for the text overlay. **No text baked in** — overlay the hook in the
  posting tool so copy stays editable per placement.
- Fidelity check (side-by-side vs product photo): card layout, artwork, Japanese text, HP130,
