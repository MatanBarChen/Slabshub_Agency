## Visual concept — "The Exhibition"

The card is treated as an artwork on display in a museum. Dark, moody gallery wall
(deep charcoal with subtle painterly texture), one warm overhead spotlight forming a
soft pool of light, thin brass frame line around the card, small empty brass plaque
below it. Ambient accents in deep teal + warm ochre (post-impressionist palette cues).
No recognizable paintings anywhere in the background — texture only. This replaces the
usual psychic-purple treatment for this card.
