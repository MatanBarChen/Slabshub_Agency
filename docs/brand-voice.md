# SlabsHub — Brand Voice & Hashtag Bank

Single reference for copywriter + publisher. Cite it, don't restate it.

## Voice

- Collector talking to collectors: fluent hobby slang (slab, pop count, grail, raw vs graded, GEM MINT), nostalgic but precise.
- Lead with the strongest concrete number ("$40 under last eBay sale", "Pop 32 in PSA 10", "one copy, no restock").
- Storytelling is welcome on FB organic (card history, why the grade matters); IG captions tighter; ads tightest.
- English by default (international audience). Hebrew variant only when requested or brief targets Israeli buyers.
- Emoji: 1–3 per post, thematic (🔥⚡🎨🧢🖼️📍), never emoji walls.

## Claims — hard rules

- Every price/market figure needs a source already cited in the brief (PriceCharting, SNKRDUNK, eBay solds + date). No source in brief → no claim in copy.
- Market history framed as history, never prediction. Banned: "investment", "guaranteed", "will go up", ROI framing.
- Scarcity = literal inventory only ("exactly one slab" must mean inventory 1). No manufactured urgency.
- No condition claims (NM/sealed/mint) beyond what the grading label or brief verifies. Raw cards: "sold as-is with full photos".
- No Pokemon IP assets beyond the actual product photo. No PSA population claims unless in the brief.
