## Spacing rules (already baked into the slot grid)
- ≥24h between posts on the same platform.
- Never same-minute FB + IG twins — adjacent slots or ≥2.5h stagger.
- Paid ad flights start only AFTER the campaign's organic posts have landed (retarget warmed traffic).

## Standard campaign shape
FB organic (opener) → +1 day IG feed (hero) → +1 day IG story → paid flight after all organic (if approved & unblocked).
