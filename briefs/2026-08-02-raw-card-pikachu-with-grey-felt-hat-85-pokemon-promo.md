## Card identity & story (verified)

Released 2023-09-28 for the Van Gogh Museum's 50th-anniversary collaboration with The Pokemon Company. Pikachu is painted in Van Gogh's own style, mirroring his 1887 self-portrait — a genuine fine-art x Pokemon crossover. The promo was given away at the museum and via Pokemon Center, distribution was cut short after scalper chaos, and it is no longer obtainable from any official source. PriceCharting describes it as "one of the most iconic modern Pokemon promo cards." Identity confirmed via PriceCharting (release date, set, collab details) and SNKRDUNK Magazine (Jun 2025).
