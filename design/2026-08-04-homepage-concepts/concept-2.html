A Note From The Curator
On grading & trust
I.
Nothing enters uncertified
Every graded piece carries a PSA or CGC cert number, published in its placard and searchable on the grader's own registry.
II.
What you see is what ships
Photography is done in-house under studio light — the image is the actual card, not a stand-in.
III.
Raw pieces, honestly described
Ungraded cards carry the same centering and surface notes a grader would record, in plain language.
