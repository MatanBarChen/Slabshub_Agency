№ 4Letters to the desk

How do I know a graded slab is authentic?

Every PSA slab we list shows its certification number. Punch it into PSA's public cert verification and you'll see the exact card, grade, and label — before you spend a shekel.

What condition are the raw cards in?

Every raw card is photographed in-hand, front and back — the photo in the listing is the exact copy you receive, not a stock image. If you want extra photos of a specific corner or edge, just ask.

How are orders packed and shipped?

Cards ship sleeved and protected, slabs padded. We're collectors — your order is packed the way we'd want our own cards to arrive.

Why is everything one copy only?

We're a curated shop, not a warehouse. Each listing is a single card we picked, photographed, and priced against recent sold listings. When it sells, it's gone — that's what the email list is for.

What about returns?

See our shipping & returns policy for the full details.
