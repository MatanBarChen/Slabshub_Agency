A letter from the editor

SlabsHub · The Graded Card Circular

Every card in this shop passed through our hands first. We pulled it, sleeved it, photographed it front and back, and priced it against what the market actually pays — not what we wish it paid.

That's why the inventory here is small and every listing is a single copy. We'd rather run a tight, honest catalog than a warehouse of stock photos. When you buy a card from SlabsHub, the photo you saw is the exact card that arrives at your door.

Got a question about a cert, a corner, or a card you're hunting? Write to us — you'll get an answer from the person who actually pulled the card.

— The SlabsHub desk
