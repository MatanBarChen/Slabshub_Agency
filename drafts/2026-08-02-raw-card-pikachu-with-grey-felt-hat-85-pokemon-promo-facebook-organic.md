**Amsterdam, September 2023.** The Van Gogh Museum turns 50, and instead of the usual anniversary exhibition merch, it partners with The Pokemon Company on something genuinely unprecedented: an official Pokemon card painted in Van Gogh's own style.

Pikachu with Grey Felt Hat #085. Illustrated by Naoyo Kimura, modeled directly on Van Gogh's 1887 "Self-Portrait with Grey Felt Hat" — the brushwork, the palette, the pose. It remains the only Pokemon card ever co-created with a major art museum.

Then the launch imploded. Crowds and scalpers overwhelmed the museum, distribution was cut short, and the card has never been obtainable from any official source since. What was meant to be a friendly giveaway became one of the defining modern promos overnight — PriceCharting describes it as one of the most iconic modern Pokemon promo cards.

That's market history, not a prediction — we sell cards, not crystal balls. But it tells you where this card sits in the modern canon: it crossed over from the TCG world into art-collecting culture, and it never came back.
